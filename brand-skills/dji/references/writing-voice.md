## 1. Source and confidence

Drawn from copy on the live dji.com homepage, re-checked on 2026-10-29 on both the desktop and mobile sites (hero carousel, product grid, "Shot on" gallery, stories, category cards, service row, footer) and from DJI's meta description. ## 2. The product-block formula

Every product slide and card on the homepage uses the same three-part block:

1. **Descriptor line** (small, above): a plain, specific category phrase.
2. **Product name** (large).
3. **Tagline** (short, imperative or evocative).
Then two CTAs: **Learn More** and **Buy Now**. Some products get **Learn More** alone (Osmo Action 6, RS 5, Matrice 400, FlyCart 100, Osmo 360, Osmo Mobile 8); Agras swaps the CTAs for per-model links ("DJI AGRAS T100", "DJI AGRAS T70P").

## 3. Taglines

- **2 to 6 words.** Title Case. Usually no final period (the enterprise "Big Drone, Big Jobs." is an exception).
- **Lead with a verb or a rhythmic noun phrase.** "Chase the View." "Lead the Scene." "Open New Possibilities." "For Moving Moments."
- **Name the creator's outcome, not the spec.** The spec lives in the descriptor line.
- **Echo the product's job in a visual or kinetic metaphor:** views, scenes, moments, motion, focus, tune, square-up. Aerial and movement vocabulary suits the brand (chase, lead, rise, frame, glide, capture).
- **Use parallel or paired structures** for rhythm: "Square Up, Nail the Move." "Big Drone, Big Jobs."
- **Enterprise and professional lines are more serious and abstract:** "Engineered for Excellence, Designed for Versatility."
- Avoid exclamation marks, questions as taglines, puns, and stacked buzzwords.

## 4. Descriptors and specs

- The descriptor is **factual and compact**: a noun phrase naming format, lens/sensor, and use. "1″ CMOS Pocket Gimbal Camera", "Triple-Lens Camera Drone".
- Lead with the differentiating spec or role: sensor size, number of lenses, resolution, tracking, endurance.
- Compound modifiers are hyphenated and often capitalized as in the brand's own style: "All-In-One", "Dual-Camera", "Long-Endurance".
- Use real units and symbols: `1″ CMOS`, `8K`, `360°`, `4K/120fps`. Don't write "high-quality sensor" when you can write the sensor size. If you don't know a spec, leave a clearly marked placeholder instead of inventing one.
- Flagship words ("Flagship", "Pro", "Creator Combo") are used for tiering, not hype.

## 5. CTAs and UI labels

- Primary pair: **Learn More** and **Buy Now**. Title Case, 2 words. On the site they are written as links followed by a chevron ("Learn More ›"), not as filled buttons.
- Other labels: Add to Cart, Where to Buy, Help Me Choose, Compare, Download App, Subscribe, Support.
- One verb, no punctuation, no exclamation marks.
- Section heads for utility links are plain nouns: "Where to buy", "Support", "Fly Safe".
- Safety and compliance copy is plain and direct ("Fly Safe"); keep warnings clear and unembellished.

## 6. Product and family naming

- Brand prefix is usually "DJI" for the formal name ("DJI Air 3S", "DJI Mavic 3 Pro", "DJI Flip") and often dropped in hero titles. Osmo and Ronin lines frequently use the family name ("Osmo Pocket 3", "Osmo Action 6", "Osmo Mobile 8").
- Families: Mavic, Air, Mini, Flip, Neo, Avata (drones); Osmo Pocket, Action, Mobile, Nano, 360 (handheld); RS, Ronin (stabilizers); Mic (audio); Matrice, Inspire, Zenmuse (professional/enterprise); Agras (agriculture); FlyCart (delivery); Power (stations).
- Keep model numbers and suffixes exact (Mini 4 Pro, Air 3S, Osmo Mobile 8P). Combos are named "Fly More Combo", "Creator Combo", "Standard Combo", "Adventure Combo".
- Don't invent product names or specs for real DJI products. For a fictional product in DJI's style, make the name clearly fictional.

## 7. Longer copy

**Section and card copy on the live homepage (2026-10-29):**

| Element | Copy |
|---------|------|
| Stories heading | Standing at the Forefront of Innovation |
| Stories line | As we explore new technology, we push the capabilities of what is possible, driving progress through continuous innovation. |
| Editorial card | Industry Insight Report · DJI Agriculture Annual Report |
| Editorial card | Engineering, Science & Technology · DJI Ronin 2 Gimbal System Honored with 2025 Scientific and Technical Award |
| Category heading | Explore DJI Products in Different Fields |
| Category cards | Video Production: Professional Aerial and Ground Filmmaking Tools · Enterprise: Drone Solutions for a New Generation of Work · Agriculture: Efficient and Intelligent Agricultural Solution |
| Utility card | Product Info · Compare Camera Drones · See product overviews and comparisons here · Help Me Choose |
| Service row | Where to buy · Support · Fly Safe |
| Newsletter | Get the latest news from DJI |

Section headings and card descriptors are Title Case with no period. Service-row labels are the exception: they are sentence case ("Where to buy"). Only the running sentence (the stories line) ends with a period.


- **Mission/editorial register:** confident, forward-looking, about exploration and creativity. "As we explore new technology, we push the capabilities of what is possible, driving progress through continuous innovation." Short declarative sentences, "we" for the company, "you" for creators.
- **Creator register:** the product frees the user to capture what was once out of reach. Focus on moments, movement, travel, and the finished image.
- **Enterprise/agri register:** operational and outcome-focused (efficiency, coverage, safety, deliveries). Numbers and workflows over feelings.
- Body copy: 1 to 2 short sentences per feature, one idea each, active voice, concrete verbs.
- "Shot on [product]" is a signature proof line for camera products.

## 9. Edit pass

1. Does the descriptor name a real format and spec, with no adjectives doing the work?
2. Is the tagline 2 to 6 words, verb-led or rhythmic, in Title Case?
3. Are CTAs exactly "Learn More" and "Buy Now" (or the shortest equivalent)?
4. Did you remove every "amazing", "revolutionary", "cutting-edge", "game-changing", and exclamation mark?
5. Are specs real, with units, or clearly marked placeholders?
6. Does the page read in one pass: descriptor, name, tagline, buttons?
